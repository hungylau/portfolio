Behind the work

Search recovery

Diagnosing a drop in organic search traffic

Comparing pre- and post-launch data in Google Search Console revealed gaps in the new site and highlighted articles that had previously performed well. Those findings helped prioritise what to rewrite and republish.

The guide to identical and non-identical twins was one example. My updates reinstated scientific abbreviations families were using in search. Changes to article titles, standfirsts and body copy were assessed against pre-update search performance.

Read the live article on Twins Trust

CMS training

Training editors to publish independently

Colleagues needed a practical reference for publishing in Wagtail. Written guidance covered common tasks, and recorded training gave editors a way to revisit the workflow. The project also included early work on an intranet for training materials, policies and shared assets.

Helpline journey

Designing the helpline’s response workflows

Expanding the Twins Trust helpline meant planning how enquiries would be handled across channels. My contribution to the virtual contact centre included considering wait times and what automated replies should tell families about when to expect a response.

The new workflows accounted for WhatsApp’s 24-hour reply window. Training for volunteers and the Family Services Officer covered how to handle enquiries across the channels.
